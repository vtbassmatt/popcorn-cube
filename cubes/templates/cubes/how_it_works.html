How it works

Each player submits one card per round until the cube is full.

  1. Submit one card each round.
  2. Starting in round 2, try to riff on cards from the previous round.
  3. Round 1 has no previous round, so just pick a starting card.
  4. You can optionally add a short reason for your pick; it is shown wherever that card is shown.

Cube-specific settings

  • Format legality: A cube can enforce a format (like Modern or Pioneer). If set, only cards legal in that format can be submitted.
  • Max copies per card: Cubes can limit copies of the same card. 1 means singleton, and 0 means unlimited copies.
